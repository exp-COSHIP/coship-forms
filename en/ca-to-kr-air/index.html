Personal Customs Clearance Code
Required for Korean customs clearance. (The Resident Registration Number may be used instead.)
PCCC: 13 characters starting with P · RRN example: 920302-1******
What is a PCCC? (How to obtain)
The PCCC is a 13-digit code (P + 12 digits) used instead of a Resident Registration Number for international shipments.
Issue/lookup free at Korea Customs UNI-PASS after identity verification.
If you do not have a PCCC, you may enter the 13-digit Korean Resident Registration Number instead.
⚠️ Enhanced Customs Verification (effective Feb 2, 2026): Korea Customs now requires that the ① recipient name, ② mobile phone registration, and ③ registered delivery address (postal code) all match the PCCC holder. Mismatched information may delay clearance until verified.

Shipping Restrictions — Canada to Korea Air
Please review each item and check the box beside it. All must be confirmed to proceed.
❌ Prohibited Items (disposed if found):
🚨 Flammable / aerosol sprays — strictly prohibited on aircraft. Heavy airline penalties apply if detected
E-cigarettes, processed meat (jerky, etc.), melatonin, animal feed, medical devices, contact lenses, tattoo machines, sexual performance products, narcotics, blades over 15cm, batteries, Tylenol
⚠️ Restricted Items:
• Agricultural / livestock products (honey, dairy, coffee, tea, nuts, grains): max 5kg per item — subject to quarantine inspection; exceeding may incur recipient-paid inspection fees or disposal
• Health supplements / medications: max 6 bottles per person (exceeding prevents clearance)
• Perfume: one 60ml bottle (if the $150 duty-free limit is exceeded, additional excise and education taxes may apply)
• Alcohol: one bottle (max 1L, under $400) (exceeding prevents clearance)
🔋 Products with Lithium Batteries (declaration required, +$10): Electric toothbrushes, shavers, scales, phones, power banks, laptops, game consoles, Bluetooth audio, remotes, cameras, smartwatches, car keys, printers, tablets, etc. — undeclared items may result in airline penalties charged to the recipient
📋 Non-Acceptable / Non-Liable Items: Cash, securities, precious metals, ID documents, glassware, and perishables cannot be accepted. Partial damage, partial loss, and electronic malfunction are excluded from compensation
