💰 When Additional Fees May Apply
The following are not included in the base rate quoted at intake:

· Remote area surcharge — for delivery to areas designated as remote/rural by the carrier
· Volumetric weight charge — billed based on volumetric weight when it exceeds actual weight
· Re-delivery fee — if a second delivery attempt is required due to recipient absence, etc.
· Address correction fee — if the carrier corrects an inaccurate address provided at intake
· Additional pickup visit — if a re-visit is needed because the shipment was not ready at the scheduled time
· Ground delivery fee — if home delivery is requested instead of destination pickup
🛡️ Coverage Terms
① Partial damage or partial loss is not covered (excluded from compensation).
② Compensation applies only for total loss, based on the declared value, up to a maximum of CAD $400.
③ No compensation is available if a declared value was not provided, and the shipper (customer) is fully responsible for any consequences of an inaccurate declared value.
📦 Packing Guidelines
Shipments may be compressed or dropped during transit. Please fill any empty space inside boxes with cushioning material and seal all sides securely with tape. Fragile items should be individually wrapped.
